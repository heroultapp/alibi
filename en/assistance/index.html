Support

A question, a bug, an idea for a case? Write to us at heroulta.app@gmail.com: we reply within 72 hours.

How do I start a game?

One player creates a room and reads out the six-character code. The others tap “Join with a code”. The host picks the two suspects and the case, then starts the game. With no network, choose “Play with a single phone”.

Do I need an account?

No. “Continue without an account” gives access to the whole game. An account (Apple, Google or e-mail) lets you find your nickname, free games and purchases on another device.

Restore my purchases

Open the Account tab, then “Restore my purchases”, with the same Apple ID used for the purchase.

Cancel the Club

iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Alibi. Access remains active until the end of the paid period.

Delete my account

Account tab › “Delete my account” › confirm. It’s immediate.

I forgot my password

Write to heroulta.app@gmail.com from your e-mail account’s address: we’ll help you get your access back.

The room won’t connect

Check that every phone has internet access (Wi-Fi or mobile data), then close and reopen the app. Reconnecting to a game in progress is automatic.
