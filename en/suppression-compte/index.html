Delete your Alibi account

You can delete your account directly in the app, in three taps, without writing to us.

From the app

  1. Open the “Account” tab at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap “Delete my account”.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

What is deleted

Your Alibi identifier, nickname, progress, the link with your Apple account (the authorization is revoked with Apple) and the game data stored on your phone.

What is kept

Your purchases stay linked to your Apple ID and can be restored. An active subscription must be cancelled separately in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Without access to the app

Write to heroulta.app@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”. We handle requests within 30 days.
