Privacy Policy

Alibi collects only what it needs to get your group playing: no ads, no tracking, no data selling. You can delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Alibi is published by Arthur Héroult, the data controller under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). For any question or request: heroulta.app@gmail.com.

2. The data we process

  • Alibi account: a random identifier, the creation date and the sign-in method (Apple or no account).
  • Sign in with Apple: the anonymous identifier Apple provides for our app and, only if you choose to share it, your first name. We do not ask for your e-mail address.
  • Nickname: the name you show to other players in a room.
  • Progress: the number of free discovery games already played, to apply the two free games.
  • Room games: the names, written answers and votes of the current game.
  • Purchases: your Alibi account identifier and the list of active collections or subscriptions, sent by Apple through RevenueCat. We never receive your payment details.
  • Security: your connection IP address, used only in memory for one hour to limit abuse. It is not stored.

3. What stays on your phone

Your profile photo, the “single phone” mode, the names typed for a local game, your sound and vibration settings and your drafts stay only on your device. They are never sent to us.

4. Why we use it

No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics or training artificial intelligence. Alibi contains no advertising or tracking tools and sells no data.

  • To run your account and multiplayer rooms (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To apply the free games and give your group access to purchased collections (performance of a contract).
  • To protect the service against abuse (legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

5. How long we keep it

  • Room answers, votes and names: in memory only, erased when the room closes or after two hours at most.
  • Account, nickname and progress: until you delete your account.
  • IP address: one hour at most, in memory.
  • Purchase history: kept by Apple and RevenueCat according to their legal obligations.

6. Who can access it

Some providers may be located outside the European Union. Such transfers are covered by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

  • The other players in your room see your nickname, then your answers when they are revealed.
  • Our hosting provider, which runs the game server on our behalf.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and payments, under its own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc., the provider that verifies your purchases. It receives neither your answers nor your nickname.

7. Deleting your account

In the app: open the “Account” tab (or tap the profile picture at the top of the home screen), then “Delete my account” and confirm. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your identifier, nickname, progress and link with Apple are erased from our servers, and the “Sign in with Apple” authorization is revoked.

Your purchases stay linked to your Apple ID: you can restore them later. An active subscription is not cancelled automatically; manage it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Without access to the app, write to heroulta.app@gmail.com from the address linked to your Apple account; we handle requests within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data. Write to us at heroulta.app@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Alibi is a party game for people aged 13 and over. Where local law requires it, younger players need a parent’s consent before creating an account. Playing without an account is always available.

10. Security

Exchanges with the server are encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Sign-in tokens are stored hashed and access to the server’s files is restricted.

11. Changes

This policy was updated on 29 September 2026. If anything important changes, the app will tell you before it applies.
