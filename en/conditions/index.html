Terms of Use

The simple rules for playing Alibi, buying collections and using your account.

1. The service

Alibi is a party game for 3 to 8 people in the same room, published by Arthur Héroult. It can be used with or without an account.

2. Your account

You can create an account with “Sign in with Apple”, with Google or with your e-mail address, or play without one. Keep your password secret. You are responsible for the nickname you display. You can delete your account at any time from the app.

3. Fair play

  • No insulting, hateful or harassing nicknames or answers.
  • No attempts to disrupt the server, get around paid access or create accounts in bulk.
  • A room’s host can remove a player. We may suspend an account that breaks these rules.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Two discovery games are free. Collections and the Pass are one-time purchases. The Alibi Club is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

The Club may start with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Nothing is charged during the trial; unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends, the chosen plan is billed automatically at the displayed price and then renews every period.

Payment is charged to your Apple account on confirmation. Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com).
